## Description (≤4000 chars)

```
Stash is the fastest way to save anything, and the easiest way to find it again.

Share a link, a screenshot, a photo, a PDF, or a voice note into Stash from any app on your phone. That's the whole capture flow: no naming, no filing, no folders, no decisions. One share-sheet tap and it's saved.

Once something is in your stash, it works for you. Links get real titles and descriptions. Voice notes get transcribed. Screenshots and documents get read, so their text is searchable. Long recordings and articles get summarized. Everything becomes something you can actually find later, not just something you meant to come back to.

Then you can ask for it back. Type a question in plain language — "that restaurant my friend sent me last month," "the pricing page I saved on Tuesday" — and Stash searches everything you've saved and answers with a link straight to the item. No scrolling through folders, no remembering what you called something.

What you can save:
• Links — Stash fetches the title, description, and preview image automatically
• Screenshots and photos — read and made searchable
• PDFs and documents — full text extracted and summarized
• Voice notes — transcribed automatically
• Plain notes — for the thought you don't want to lose

How you save it:
• The iOS share sheet, from Safari, Photos, or any app that can share
• The Stash app itself, for quick notes and voice memos
• Text a link or photo to Stash over WhatsApp or SMS
• The Stash web app and Chrome extension, if you also use Stash on your computer

How you find it again:
• Search across everything you've saved
• Ask Stash a question and get an answer with a link to the source item

Stash is free to download; an active Stash subscription from gostash.it is needed to save new items. Manage your subscription anytime at gostash.it.

Your stash is private by default. Nothing you save is used to train AI models, shown to other people, or sold.
```
