Reminders on the card (C, chosen)

One reminder chip, top-left of the object zone, on white glass so it reads on a photo, a dark plate or a paper field alike. It escalates as the date nears: a quiet clock five days out, the interactive violet at three, a violet ring the day before, a solid violet fill on the day. What each exercise asks for, and what it gives up

A · Tidy

  • Every card gets the same meta line: source and date, sentence case, no tracking. The kicker stops being a links-only thing.
  • Type identity moves from the chip to the source word (tinted glyph + word for voice / document / screenshot) — DESIGN.md's "tinted type chip always visible" becomes "tinted type word always visible".
  • Title 22/1.18 (from 20/1.25); description clamps at 2 (from 3).
  • Chips row deleted. The one salient fact is plain footer text; format · size and the menu are hover-only. The date leaves the footer for the meta line.
  • Gives up: nothing structural — heroes and box stay. A one-line-title card still sits shorter than its neighbour.

B · One voice

  • One family: Neue Montreal, weight as hierarchy (600 title / 400 body). The serif card title is retired.
  • No box: image, text, whitespace on a quiet white page; 40 / 56px gutters do the separating. The gradient wash has to go quiet under it (toggle Borderless off to see why).
  • A 20px tile leads the meta line: the site's favicon for links, a tinted Lucide glyph for captured media — colour still means type.
  • Footer reads like Medium's stats row: fact with icon at rest; format · size and the menu on hover.
  • Gives up: the one serif moment, the card box, and the page wash behind the grid.

C · Uniform frame

  • A's typography inside one silhouette: every type gets a 176px object zone — notes render their own words on a neutral field, social posts their pull-quote, voice notes a full-height player, repos a plate with stats.
  • Title and description are fixed two-line boxes, so every title in a row starts at the same y and every footer sits at the same height.
  • Notes gain the AI description under their words; an annotation replaces the description rather than stacking on it.
  • Reminder chip on the hero, top-left, white glass: far = clock + ink 72%, near (≤3 days) = bell + violet text, the day before = violet ring, due = violet fill. Snooze / Dismiss on rollover (touch: tap the chip for a sheet). Replaces the footer chip and the top-right "Due" pill; labels read "in 3 days", not "in 3d".
  • Gives up: a one-line title leaves a blank line; a card can never show more than it has room for, even when the neighbours are short.
