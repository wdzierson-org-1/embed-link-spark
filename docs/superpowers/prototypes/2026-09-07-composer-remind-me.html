Remind me, from the composer.

The reminder contract already exists: capture endpoints accept an optional remind_at, the presets are 1 / 3 / 5 days, and the iOS share sheet has its chips above Save. The web capture box has no control yet. Three ways to add one, each entirely optional: nothing on the composer changes until someone reaches for it, and a capture without a reminder is exactly what it is today.

1 · A bell beside the pin persistent tool, mirrors the location toggle

The composer already has one optional add-on with exactly this shape: the location pin. It is a round outline button that takes the violet tint when on, and writes a small caption beside itself ("posted from Kure Beach"). A bell that does the same is zero new vocabulary. Pressing it opens the three presets; once set, the bell stays lit and the caption says when the card comes back.

2 · A reminder is something you attach contextual, appears once there is a capture to attach it to

The composer already grows a chip row under the editor when you paste a link or drop a file. A reminder can live in that same row as one more thing attached to this capture. At rest there is nothing; once there is content, a quiet dashed "Remind me" appears in the row. It expands into the presets in place, and settles into a chip beside the attachments.

3 · Say it in the note zero chrome, works on every capture channel

No control at all. If the words "remind me in 3 days" (or "tomorrow", "in a week", "next Friday") appear in what you type, the composer recognises the phrase, tints it, and tells you what it will do. The parse can run server-side at capture, which means it also works from SMS, WhatsApp, the extension and the share sheet with no UI on any of them. Lowest friction of the three; also the least discoverable, so it pairs with 1 or 2 rather than replacing them.

Whatever the entry point, the result is the same C card with its reminder chip. Option 1's caption, option 2's chip and option 3's token all write the same remind_at.

Recommendation

Ship 1 and 3 together. The bell mirrors the location pin so it costs nothing to learn and is visible before anyone has typed; the phrase parser is the friction-free path and, run at capture, it lights up SMS, WhatsApp, the extension and the share sheet for free. Option 2 is the most elegant on the web but hides the affordance until there is content, and it competes with the input-chip row for space on a narrow composer.

  • The composer posts remind_at alongside the capture (already accepted by every capture endpoint; invalid values are ignored). No new endpoint.
  • The parser is a small regex family on the note text at submit (web) and on content at capture (server): "remind me in N days", "tomorrow", "in a week", "next <weekday>". Anything it cannot parse is left alone. Whole-note phrases are consumed; in-sentence phrases are kept.
  • Presets stay 1 / 3 / 5 in the bell; the parser can express any day. Custom dates in the UI remain out of scope, as the spec says.
  • Both write the same card state: the chip on the hero, escalating as the date nears.
