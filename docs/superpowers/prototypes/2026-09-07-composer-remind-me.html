Remind me, wherever you capture.

Sep 7, decided. The "Remind me" chip is always offered, on every capture surface, and it expands to the right into the presets the way the card's reminder chip expands into Snooze and Dismiss. Typing something reminder-shaped tints the phrase and pre-fills the chip; otherwise the chip just waits, and the person can reach for it themselves. The × is the only remove control. iOS composer and share sheet go as shown in sections 3 and 4.

Why it is there. Stash is not a reminder app. The chip is a convenience and a way back to something, and a reminder is an explicit signal that this object matters to the person who saved it, which the email digest can use to decide what to resurface.

1 · The chip row chosen · always offered a reminder is something you attach

The composer already grows a chip row under the editor when you paste a link or drop a file. A reminder lives in that same row as one more thing attached to this capture, and unlike v0.2 the offer is there from the start: a quiet dashed "Remind me" sits under the empty editor, one decision that never has to be discovered. It unfolds to the right into the presets, in place, and settles into a chip beside the attachments. Typing a phrase (section 2) fills the very same chip. The row costs the idle composer one line; that is the price of always offering.

2 · Say it in the note chosen the recognizer, live — type into it

The chip is always there; the recognizer only makes it smarter. Being smart means two questions, not one: is this person asking to be brought back to something, and when. Intent comes from a lexicon of reminder language, graded strong or weak; time comes from a small grammar of everyday date phrases. The two combine into one of four actions, in the table on the right; when the words are not reminder-shaped the chip simply waits. Nothing here is a model: a deterministic regex family that ports to TypeScript (web), Swift (iOS, share sheet) and the capture edge functions (SMS, WhatsApp, extension) with no drift.

Intent × time → action

IntentTimeAction
strongyesset — chip filled with the resolved day; × to remove
strongnoneset, default tomorrow — presets shown with 1 day selected so changing is one tap

weaknoneoffer — presets unfold, nothing selected, Save is untouched
noneyesnothing — "team offsite friday" is a fact, not a request

Consumed or kept. When the whole note is the phrase and something is attached (a link, a file, a share), the phrase is an instruction: it is dropped and the capture saves with a reminder and no annotation. When the phrase sits inside a sentence, the words stay; the tint is composer-only and is never stored. The × removes the reminder and the tint; the words stay in either case.

3 · On iOS: the composer chosen as shown Add tab, same row inside the card

The in-app composer card gets the identical row between the text and the icon bar. Same offer, same unfold, same chip, same × — and the same recognizer running on the text, so the two surfaces never disagree about what a phrase means. The dashed chip deliberately looks nothing like the round icon buttons beneath it: one less thing to tell apart. The icon bar itself is a lot of buttons; simplifying it is a separate, later pass.

4 · On iOS: the share sheet chosen as shown where most captures happen

The share sheet is a one-shot surface: the shared item is the content, so there is no "once there is content" moment to wait for. The reminders spec already puts a preset row above Save (default off, never gating Save); v0.2 keeps that row and wires the recognizer to the note field. Type "remind me next friday" in the optional note and the row fills with that day; the note, being nothing but the instruction, is consumed. Done copy confirms: "Saved · back Fri, Sep 11".

5 · A bell beside the pin not chosen kept for the record

v0.1's option 1: a round bell button mirroring the location toggle, with a presets popover and a "back in 3 days" caption. Discoverable, but it puts a permanent control on a composer whose whole point is that nothing on it asks for attention, and it gives the reminder a second home away from the chip row. What to build

One recognizer, three ports, one contract. The lexicon and grammar above become a small pure module in src/utils/reminderPhrase.ts (web composer), StashKit/ReminderPhrase.swift (iOS composer + share extension) and a shared Deno module for the capture edge functions, each with the same fixture table so a phrase means the same thing everywhere.

  • Clients run it first. Web and iOS parse as you type, show the row, and always send the remind_at key on capture — an ISO time when set, null when the user removed it with ×. The server parses content only when the key is absent (SMS, WhatsApp, extension, MCP), so an × on the client is never overridden.
  • Time is resolved on the device (local midnight + 9 am for day-grained phrases; 6 pm for "later today"). Server-side parses for phone channels need a timezone the contract does not carry yet; until it does, the server resolves in UTC and the digest job's 24 h due window absorbs the slack. Flagging, not solving, here.
  • Presets stay 1 / 3 / 5 in the row; the recognizer may express any day and the chip shows the absolute date whenever the phrase was not a preset. Custom dates in the UI remain out of scope, as the spec says.
  • Share sheet: the spec's share.remind.1d/3d/5d row above Save, plus the recognizer on share.note; whole-note phrases are consumed. Done copy gains the day. Hidden with the rest of the bar when the account gate is closed.
  • iOS composer was "later" in the spec; the row is the same component as the share sheet's, so it comes along.
  • A reminder is a signal, not just a timer. Setting one says this object matters; the email digest (reminders plan 3) should treat items with a reminder, set or cleared, as candidates to resurface, and the spec's decision to keep reminder_cleared_at rather than deleting means the history stays queryable for that.
  • Every entry point writes the same card state: the chip on the hero, escalating as the date nears (card prototype, v0.2).
