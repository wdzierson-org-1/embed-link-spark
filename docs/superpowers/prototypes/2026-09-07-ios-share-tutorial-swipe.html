Three-panel share tutorial

Post-sign-in screen (replaces/extends HowToStashView's current single panel). Same paper-card shape and real step captures — two layouts to compare before implementing.

Variant

Try it

  1. Carousel: drag the panel, use ← → arrow keys, tap a dot, or hit Next. Next becomes Got it on the last panel.
  2. Columns: the shipped v1 layout — all three steps visible at once, no interaction needed.

Tradeoff

Carousel gives each step full-size art (easier to actually read the share-sheet row) but costs a swipe most users will blow past. Columns is scannable in one glance but the images are thumbnail-small — legible on a big phone, tight on SE.
