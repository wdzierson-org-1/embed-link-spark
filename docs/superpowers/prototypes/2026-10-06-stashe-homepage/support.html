report content or a user
Something on a public feed, in a comment or at a share link that shouldn’t be there

Abusive, illegal, or someone’s private information: send us the link, or the username, and what’s wrong. It goes to the people who make Stash, who decide what to do under the Terms.

delete your account
Settings → Your information → Delete account

On the web, or under Settings in the iPhone app. It is immediate and permanent: everything you saved, your profile, your sign-in tokens and your subscription go with it. What stays, and for how long, is in the privacy policy. If the button fails for you, write to us.
