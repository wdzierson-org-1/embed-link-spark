Sign in once with your Stash account. After that, saving is one click.

No account yet? Create one at gostash.it.

Signed in as — you're all set.

  • Click the Stash button in the toolbar to save the page you're on.
  • Right-click selected text and choose Stash it to save it as a note.
  • Right-click any image and choose Stash it to save the image itself.

A small ✓ appears on the toolbar icon when a save lands. Add context to anything later at gostash.it.
