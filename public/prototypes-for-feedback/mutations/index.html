Try it

  1. On the paper, tap Flashcards. The deck is a new object, stitched to its source. Tap the card to flip it; arrow for the next one.
  2. On the headphones, start a price watch (a standing mutation), then Find similar.
  3. Tap Custom… to see where make-your-own fits.
  4. The last scene is the digest — where offers arrive on their own.

The contract

Capture stays sacred. No mutation is ever offered while saving.

Outputs are new objects. The save is immutable; the derived thing links back to it.

Standing mutations ride the digest. Price watches and review schedules use the same rail as resurfacing.
